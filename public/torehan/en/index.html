3 things Torehan! does for you

Can't read the circle names? You don't have to.

1Circle and item names, translated into your language

Circle names, creator names and the items on each circle's list are almost always written in Japanese. Tap Translate and they're shown in your app language — so you know what you're lining up for before you join the queue. Prices stay in yen, exactly as listed.

Example (for illustration)

新刊セット ¥1,500→New release set ¥1,500
アクリルスタンド ¥1,200→Acrylic stand ¥1,200
既刊 ¥500→Back issue ¥500

Translation is optional and only runs when you tap Translate. It uses AI provided by Anthropic: circle names, creator names and item names are sent to it to be translated. The first 10 circles are free, then ¥100 for 50 more circles. Translations are saved on your device, so viewing a circle again doesn't use your count, and the Japanese original is kept.

Menus, buttons, guides — all in your language

2The whole app speaks your language

Not just the store page: every screen — menus, buttons, settings, the Comiket guide and the packing checklist — is available in your language.Circle information comes from Japanese sources, so use the translation above for circle and item names. Prices are shown in yen.

No more wandering the halls looking for a circle

3Venue maps and budget, in one place

See where each circle is on hall-by-hall venue maps; pinch to zoom and mark circles right on the map. Your list adds up the total you'll need (in yen), and you can check off what you've bought so nothing gets missed.

And on the day, even easier

From paying and splitting up with friends to research and packing.

📷 Read item lists from a photo

Take a photo of a circle's item list, and the names and prices are read straight into your list and budget.AI reading is optional — you can always enter items by hand.

🧮 Change calculator

Enter the total and what you're handing over, and your change is worked out instantly. No panicked mental maths at the table.

🤝 Split with friends

Create a group with an invite code and share out the circles between members. Money you advance for each other's purchases is settled automatically.

📚 First time at Comiket?

How to use the app, a Comiket guide with tips on umbrellas, rolling suitcases and the best stations to get off at, and a packing checklist — all included.
